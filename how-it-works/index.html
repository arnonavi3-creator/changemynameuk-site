How it works

We prepare the paperwork and the plan. You check, sign and send everything yourself.

  1. Check what you needAnswer six quick questions in our free checker. It tells you whether you need a deed poll and which passport route fits your wedding and travel dates.
  2. Choose a plan and upload your documentsYour marriage or civil partnership certificate and your current ID. Save and come back at any time.
  3. We prepare your packYour passport and DVLA forms, a plan in the right order, and a checklist with letters for every organisation. On Guided and above, a person checks it all first.
  4. You check, sign and sendYou stay in control. We never submit anything for you, and we send reminders until every item is ticked off.

What happens in what order

The order matters, because some organisations ask to see photo ID in your new name. This is the order we usually suggest:

  1. Before the wedding: decide which name to travel in. If you want a passport in your new name for the honeymoon, you can apply up to 3 months before the wedding. Read about the honeymoon passport.
  2. After the wedding: get your marriage or civil partnership certificate, and consider ordering an extra copy, as some organisations want to see an original.
  3. Passport, if you didn't change it before the wedding.
  4. Driving licence and car log book with DVLA. Both are free.
  5. HMRC, the electoral register and your GP.
  6. Employer, banks and everything else on your checklist.

What we need from you

  • Your marriage or civil partnership certificate (a clear photo or scan is fine for us).
  • Your current passport and driving licence, if you have them.
  • Your wedding date and any travel dates.
  • The name you're changing to.

Where AI and people fit in

AI reads your documents, fills in the forms and builds your checklist. On Guided and above, a person on our team checks every packet before it reaches you. We tell you clearly which parts were prepared by AI.

What we don't do

  • We don't send anything to the government for you, and using us doesn't speed up any government process.
  • We don't give legal or immigration advice. If your name change affects a visa, please speak to a regulated immigration adviser.
