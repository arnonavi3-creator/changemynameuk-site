Common questions

Is the checklist free?

Yes. You don't need to sign up, and your answers stay on your device. Get your checklist.

Are you part of the government?

No. Change My Name UK is independent. The checklist links to the official gov.uk pages wherever it can.

Do I need a deed poll?

Usually not. Read our guide or use the free checklist.

What's the £7 voucher for?

We're researching how people change their name. If you do a 15 minute call with us about how it's going, we'll send you a £7 Amazon voucher as thanks. Book a call.

What's the beta?

We're testing a service that does the legwork for you. Find out more and apply.

Can you help with visas or immigration?

No. Please speak to an adviser regulated by the Immigration Advice Authority.

I live in Scotland or Northern Ireland. Does the checklist work for me?

Yes. It asks where you live and adjusts the steps, for example for DVA in Northern Ireland and deed polls in Scotland.

How do I delete my details?

Email us and we'll delete anything you've sent us. Your checklist answers are only on your device, so clearing your browser's site data removes them. See our privacy notice.
