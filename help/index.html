Common questions

Are you part of the government?

No. We're an independent private company with no link to any government body. Everything we help with can be done for free directly. See how.

When do you launch?

We're planning to launch in spring 2027. Join the waitlist for an early-bird discount.

Do I need a deed poll?

Usually not. Read our guide or use the free checker.

Do you send forms for me?

No. You check, sign and send everything yourself.

Can I get a refund?

You can cancel within 14 days of buying. If a form we prepared is rejected because of our mistake, we fix it free. See our terms.

What happens to my documents?

We store them securely and delete them after your case closes. Our full privacy notice will be published before launch.

Can you help with visas or immigration?

No. Please speak to an adviser regulated by the Immigration Advice Authority.

I live in Scotland or Northern Ireland. Can you help?

Yes. The passport, DVLA and HMRC steps are the same across the UK. If you need a deed poll in Scotland, we'll point you to the right place.

Complaints

If something has gone wrong, email us with "Complaint" in the subject line. We'll acknowledge it within 2 working days and aim to resolve it within 14 days.
