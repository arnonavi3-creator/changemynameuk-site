Do I need a deed poll?

Your deed poll, done properly

A personalised adult deed poll for England and Wales, worded to meet HM Passport Office requirements, checked by a person and posted to you ready to sign. £19, all in.

Most people changing their name after marriage don't need a deed poll. If you're taking your partner's surname, or double-barrelling it, your marriage or civil partnership certificate is enough. And if you do need one, you can write it yourself for free. See when you need one, and how to make your own.

When a deed poll is the right document

  • You're creating a new surname, such as blending two surnames into one.
  • You're changing your first name or middle names.
  • You're moving your old surname into your middle names.
  • You want a spelling that's different from your certificate.
  • You're changing your name for a reason other than marriage or divorce.

Questions

Is this an official government document?

No. A deed poll is a legal document you make yourself, by declaring your new name and signing it in front of a witness. No private company is part of the government, us included. We prepare the document so it's right first time.

Will my passport and driving licence be changed with it?

HM Passport Office and DVLA accept an unenrolled deed poll when it's worded and signed correctly. You still need to apply to each one yourself, and pay their fees. Your checklist explains each step.

Who can be my witness?

An adult who knows you and isn't a relative, your partner, or someone you live with. They need to watch you sign, then sign and add their name, address and occupation. Our deed poll has space for two witnesses, because some organisations prefer two.

How many copies will I need?

Most organisations want to see an original, not a photocopy, and they post it back. Three printed copies lets you send to a few places at once. You can print more from your PDF and sign each one with your witness, and every signed copy is an original.

Are there any rules about what my new name can be?

Your new name can't be offensive, mislead people (for example by suggesting a title you don't hold), or be used to avoid a debt or commit fraud. You need at least one first name and a surname, and you can use letters, hyphens and apostrophes, but not numbers or symbols.

Can I change my name more than once?

Yes. There's no limit, but each change needs its own deed poll, and organisations may ask to see the earlier ones.

I'm not a British citizen. Can I still use one?

Yes, if you live in England or Wales. If you have a passport from another country, check with that country's embassy as well, since its rules may be different. Your UK visa or residence documents also need updating, through the Home Office.

What if I made a mistake in my details?

We send you a proof to check before we print. If something's still wrong when it arrives, tell us and we'll fix and resend it for free.
