About me

Changing your name should take an afternoon, not months of forms, phone queues and guesswork. I'm starting with a free checklist, and talking to as many people as I can about what would make it easier.

Why I'm building this

Hi, my name is Avi Arnon, and I can't wait to marry the love of my life. We're both on our second act, and when we talked about our joint family name, we realised that neither of us had actually finished changing, or re-changing, our previous surnames in all the different places.

So I set out to build a simple tool that helps people start their joint life in the simplest way possible. I'd love to help you too.

Avi

What we believe

  • Be honest about free. You can change your name for free with almost every organisation, and our checklist shows you how.
  • Don't push what people don't need. Most people don't need a deed poll, so we say so.
  • Learn from real people. We'd love to hear how your name change is going. Book a 15 minute call.
