Why it keeps turning up

The typical marriage that ends in divorce in England and Wales lasts 13 years. That's 13 years of one surname going onto everything: your payslip, mortgage, pension, school forms, prescriptions, loyalty cards and logins. Most people have 25 to 40 places to update.

So people change a few, run out of energy, and stop. Then the letters keep coming. In a 2026 Opinium poll, 63% of divorced Brits still had their married name.

The fix isn't more effort. It's a complete list, done in the right order, once.

The order that works after a divorce

After a wedding, you start with your passport. After a divorce, you can't: the Passport Office wants to see you're already using your name. So you go the other way round.

  1. Start using your nameTell your employer and your council first. A payslip or council letter in your own name becomes your proof.
  2. Then your bankMost will accept your final order, marriage certificate and birth certificate together.
  3. Then your passportWith a signed statement that you've gone back to your name "for all purposes", plus proof you're using it.
  4. Then everything elseDVLA, HMRC, your GP, your pension, insurance, utilities, and that dentist.

Questions

Do I need a deed poll to go back to my maiden name?

Not usually, once your divorce is final. Your final order, marriage certificate and birth certificate are normally enough. If your divorce isn't final yet, or you want a name you've never had before, you'll need a deed poll. See our deed poll page.

Why can't I start with my passport?

HM Passport Office asks for evidence that you're already using your previous name, such as a payslip or a letter from your council, plus a signed statement that you've gone back to it for all purposes. So update your employer and council first.

Is there a time limit?

No. You can go back to your name the month after your divorce or ten years later.

Can I change from Mrs to Ms?

Yes, whenever you like. A title isn't part of your legal name, and changing it costs nothing. Just tell the places that use it.

I've lost my marriage certificate. What now?

You can order a copy from the register office where you married, or from the General Register Office. More on certificate copies.

How long does it take?

Most places update within a few days to a few weeks. The passport takes longest. With a complete list and the right order, most people can finish in a couple of months, instead of finding stragglers for years.
