What we'll do

  1. Find every place that has your nameFrom a short call or form, plus the accounts you tell us about, we build your full list: government, banks, work, pensions, insurance, home and the rest.
  2. Prepare every form and letterPassport and DVLA forms filled in, and a change of name letter for each organisation that needs one, ready to sign.
  3. Contact organisations for you, where they allow itSome organisations only accept a change from you, and the passport office and DVLA need your own signature and original documents. For those, we get everything ready so you just sign and post.
  4. Track it until every record is doneWe keep a live list, chase anything that's stuck, and remind you when something needs you.

What we ask in return: honest feedback, including a short call during and after. Beta places are limited.
