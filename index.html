Prefer to do it yourself? It's free.

You don't need us to change your name. Everything we help with can be done directly, using your marriage or civil partnership certificate:

  • Passport: gov.uk/changing-passport-information (you pay the normal passport fee)
  • Driving licence: gov.uk/change-name-driving-licence (free)
  • HMRC: gov.uk/tell-hmrc-change-of-details (free)
  • Electoral register: gov.uk/register-to-vote (free)

Our free step-by-step guide covers the rest. What we add is the time saved, the honeymoon passport check, a human review and one list that tracks every organisation until you're done.

Questions

Are you part of the government?

No. We're an independent private company. We have no link to HM Passport Office, DVLA, HMRC or any government body, and using us doesn't speed up any government process.

Do I need a deed poll?

Usually not. Taking your spouse's surname, or joining both with a hyphen, normally only needs your marriage or civil partnership certificate. A deed poll is needed for things like a new combined surname or changing a first name. Read the guide.

Do you send things to the government for me?

No. We prepare your paperwork and your plan. You check, sign and send everything yourself, so you're always in control.

Is this only for brides?

Not at all. It's for anyone changing their name after a marriage or civil partnership, including double-barrelled names and partners who both change.

Do you use AI?

Yes. AI helps fill in the forms and build your checklist. On Guided and above, a person reviews every packet before it goes to you.

Can you help with visas or immigration?

No. We don't give legal or immigration advice. If your name change affects a visa or eVisa, please speak to a regulated immigration adviser.
