## Do I have to change my name?

No. Changing your name after marriage is a choice, not a legal requirement, and it applies to anyone of any gender. You can keep your name, take your partner's, double-barrel both, or use different names at work and at home. [Your options](/guides/keeping-your-maiden-name/).

## Do I need a deed poll?

Usually not. If you're taking your partner's surname, or joining both surnames, your marriage certificate is the evidence organisations ask for. You only need a deed poll for things like a new combined surname or a first name change. [Check if you need one](/guides/do-i-need-a-deed-poll/).

## Step 1: before the wedding, decide which name to travel in

If you're going abroad soon after the wedding, your tickets must match the name on the passport you travel with. You can book in your current name and travel on your current passport, or apply for a passport in your new name up to 3 months before the wedding. [Read the honeymoon passport guide](/guides/honeymoon-passport/).

## Step 2: get your certificate

You'll usually receive your marriage certificate on the day. Some organisations want to see an original, so it's worth ordering an extra copy or two from the register office.

## Step 3: your passport

Apply for a new passport in your new name. An adult passport costs £102 online or £115.50 by post. You'll send your certificate and your old passport. [Passport guide](/guides/passport-name-change-after-marriage/).

## Step 4: your driving licence and car log book

Changing the name on your licence is free and you must do it by law. You do it by post with form D1. If you own a car, update your V5C log book too. [Driving licence guide](/guides/driving-licence-name-change/).

## Step 5: HMRC, the electoral register and your GP

- <b>HMRC:</b> update your name online in your personal tax account or the HMRC app. Your National Insurance number stays the same.
- <b>Electoral register:</b> register again with your new name. Banks use the register to check your identity.
- <b>GP:</b> tell your surgery so your NHS records are updated. Your NHS number stays the same.

## Step 6: everyone else

Your employer and payroll, banks, cards, pensions, insurance, mortgage, utilities, council tax, TV Licence, mobile, airlines and loyalty schemes. Most people have 25 to 40 to tell. [See the full checklist](/guides/who-to-tell-checklist/).

## How long does it take?

The passport usually takes a few weeks. Most other organisations take a few minutes each online or by phone, but some need a letter and your certificate by post. Spread it over a few weekends and it's very manageable.
