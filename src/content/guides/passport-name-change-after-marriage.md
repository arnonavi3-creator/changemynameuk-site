## What it costs

An adult passport costs £102 if you apply online, or £115.50 by post. You pay this directly to HM Passport Office. Paying a private business, including us, doesn't change the fee or speed up your application.

## What you need

- Your marriage or civil partnership certificate. Send the original or a certified copy from the register office, not a photocopy.
- Your current passport.
- A new digital photo (online) or two printed photos (by post).
- A deed poll as well, only if your new name isn't shown by your certificate. [Do I need one?](/guides/do-i-need-a-deed-poll/)

## How to apply

<ol class="plain">
<li>Start your application at <a href="https://www.gov.uk/changing-passport-information" rel="noopener">gov.uk/changing-passport-information</a>.</li>
<li>Enter your new name exactly as you want it to appear.</li>
<li>Post your certificate and old passport when asked. They'll be returned to you.</li>
</ol>

## How long it takes

HM Passport Office says to allow 3 weeks, and longer if they need more information. Don't book travel in your new name until the new passport arrives. [Travelling soon after the wedding?](/guides/honeymoon-passport/)

## Before the wedding

You can apply up to 3 months before the wedding with forms PD1 and PD2. Your current passport is cancelled when you apply. [Read the honeymoon passport guide](/guides/honeymoon-passport/).
