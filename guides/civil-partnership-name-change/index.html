How to change your name after a civil partnership

Changing your name after a civil partnership works the same way as after a marriage. Your civil partnership certificate is the evidence organisations ask for.

What you can change to

You can take your partner's surname, double-barrel both surnames, or keep your own. Either partner can change, or both. A new combined surname, or a first name change, needs a deed poll. When you need a deed poll.

The steps

  1. Decide which name to travel in if you have a trip coming up. Honeymoon passport guide.
  2. Get your civil partnership certificate, and consider an extra copy.
  3. Apply for a new passport. Passport guide.
  4. Update your driving licence and car log book, both free. Driving licence guide.
  5. Tell HMRC, the electoral register and your GP.
  6. Work through everyone else. Who to tell checklist.

Is anything different?

Not for the name change itself. Wherever a form or organisation mentions a marriage certificate, a civil partnership certificate is accepted in the same way. If you're asked for a "marriage certificate" and it isn't clear, ask them to confirm. They should accept it.
