How to change the name on your passport after marriage

You change the name on your passport by applying for a new one in your new name. You'll need your marriage or civil partnership certificate and your current passport.

What it costs

An adult passport costs £102 if you apply online, or £115.50 by post. You pay this directly to HM Passport Office. Paying a private company, including us, doesn't change the fee or speed up your application.

What you need

  • Your marriage or civil partnership certificate. Send the original or a certified copy from the register office, not a photocopy.
  • Your current passport.
  • A new digital photo (online) or two printed photos (by post).
  • A deed poll as well, only if your new name isn't shown by your certificate. Do I need one?

How to apply

  1. Start your application at gov.uk/changing-passport-information.
  2. Enter your new name exactly as you want it to appear.
  3. Post your certificate and old passport when asked. They'll be returned to you.

How long it takes

HM Passport Office says to allow 3 weeks, and longer if they need more information. Don't book travel in your new name until the new passport arrives. Travelling soon after the wedding?

Before the wedding

You can apply up to 3 months before the wedding with forms PD1 and PD2. Your current passport is cancelled when you apply. Read the honeymoon passport guide.
