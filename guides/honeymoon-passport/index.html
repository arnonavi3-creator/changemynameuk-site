Honeymoon passport: which name should you book in?

The one rule: the name on your tickets must match the name on the passport you travel with. You have two ways to make that work.

Option 1: travel in your current name

Book everything in your current name, travel on your current passport, and change your name when you're back. This is the simplest option and works for most people.

  • Book flights, hotels and travel insurance in the name on your current passport.
  • Check your passport is valid for long enough for where you're going. Some countries need it to be valid for several months after you arrive.
  • Don't send off your passport to change your name until you're home.

Option 2: get a passport in your new name before the wedding

You can apply for a passport in your new name up to 3 months before the wedding, using forms PD1 and PD2. The person conducting your ceremony (the registrar, or the minister for a religious wedding) fills in form PD2.

  • The new passport is dated from the wedding day, and you can't use it before then.
  • Your current passport is cancelled when you apply, so you can't travel abroad between applying and the wedding.
  • Book your honeymoon in your new name.
  • The forms are paper only, so contact the Passport Adviceline to request them.

Common mistakes

  • Booking flights in your new name and then travelling on your old passport.
  • Sending your passport off straight after the wedding when you're flying out a week later.
  • Forgetting that a hen or stag trip abroad counts as travel.
