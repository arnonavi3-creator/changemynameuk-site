Who to tell when you change your name: the checklist

Most people have 25 to 40 organisations to tell. Work through them roughly in this order, because some ask for photo ID in your new name.

1. Government and ID

  • HM Passport Office (passport)
  • DVLA (driving licence, and V5C log book if you own a car)
  • HMRC
  • Your local council: electoral register and council tax
  • DWP, if you get any benefits or the State Pension
  • Student Loans Company, if you have a student loan
  • UKVI, if you have an eVisa (speak to a regulated adviser if you're unsure about anything)

2. Work and money

  • Your employer and payroll
  • Banks and building societies
  • Credit cards and loans
  • Mortgage lender or landlord
  • Workplace and private pensions
  • ISAs, investments and Premium Bonds
  • Professional bodies and memberships

3. Health

  • Your GP surgery
  • Your dentist and optician
  • Private health insurance

4. Insurance

  • Car, home, life and travel insurance
  • Pet insurance and breakdown cover

5. Home

  • Energy and water
  • Broadband, TV and TV Licensing
  • Mobile phone

6. Everything else

  • Airlines and frequent flyer schemes
  • Supermarket and loyalty cards
  • Online shopping and subscriptions
  • Your will, if you have one, and any lasting power of attorney
  • Gym, clubs and season tickets
  • Email address and social media, if you want to

Tips

  • Keep your certificate somewhere safe. Some organisations ask for the original by post, so an extra copy from the register office helps.
  • Ask each organisation how they want to be told: app, online, phone, branch or letter.
  • Tick each one off as it's confirmed, not when you send it.
