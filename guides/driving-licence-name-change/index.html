How to change the name on your driving licence and log book

Updating your driving licence is free, and you must do it by law when your name changes. If you own a car, you need to update your V5C log book too.

Your driving licence

You can't change your name on your licence online. You do it by post:

  1. Get form D1 from a Post Office that offers DVLA services, or order it from DVLA.
  2. Fill it in with your new name.
  3. Send it with your current photocard licence and your marriage or civil partnership certificate (or deed poll) to DVLA.

It's free. You can be fined up to £1,000 if you don't tell DVLA about a change of name. You can keep driving while DVLA processes your application.

Your car log book (V5C)

If a vehicle is registered in your name, fill in the change of name and address section of your V5C, sign it and post it to DVLA. It's free, and you'll get a new log book. If you don't have your log book, apply for a new one.

Don't forget

  • Your car insurer, as the policy should be in your current legal name.
  • Any breakdown cover and parking permits.
