Married abroad? Changing your name in the UK

If you married outside the UK, you can usually use your foreign marriage certificate to change your name in the UK, in the same way as a UK certificate.

Your certificate

  • If it isn't in English (or Welsh), most organisations will ask for a certified translation alongside the original.
  • Some organisations may ask for an apostille or other proof that the certificate is genuine.
  • Check that your names are spelt the same on your certificate as on your passport. Differences can cause delays.

Your passport

HM Passport Office accepts foreign marriage certificates. Follow the normal process at gov.uk/changing-passport-information and include a certified translation if needed.

If you have a visa or eVisa

If you're not a British or Irish citizen, your name change may affect your immigration status records. We don't give immigration advice. Please speak to an adviser regulated by the Immigration Advice Authority, or check gov.uk.

What we can help with

We can prepare your UK paperwork and checklist. We don't translate certificates, and we'll tell you if your case needs specialist help first.
