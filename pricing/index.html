What's not included

  • Government fees, such as the passport fee (£102 online or £115.50 by post for an adult). You pay these directly to the government.
  • Postage, and extra copies of your marriage certificate.
  • Sending forms for you. You check, sign and send everything yourself.
  • Legal or immigration advice, or translating certificates from weddings abroad.

Our promise

If a form we prepared is rejected because of a mistake we made, we fix it free. If you change your mind, you can cancel within 14 days of buying. If we've already started your pack at your request, we may keep a fair amount for the work done. See our terms.
