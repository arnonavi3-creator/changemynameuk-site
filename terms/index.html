Terms and cancellation

Who we are

Change My Name UK is a trading name of Paperlight Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number to be added).

What we do

We prepare name change paperwork, a personal plan and a checklist, using the information and documents you give us. We don't give legal or immigration advice, we don't submit anything to the government for you, and we're not linked to any government body. You can do everything we help with yourself, for free.

Prices

The price shown is the full price, including VAT where it applies. Government fees, such as the passport fee, are paid by you directly to the government.

Your right to cancel

You can cancel within 14 days of buying, for any reason, by emailing us. If you asked us to start your pack within those 14 days, we may keep a fair amount for the work already done. Once your pack is complete and delivered, you can no longer cancel.

Deed poll add-on

Our £19 deed poll add-on is an unenrolled deed poll that we prepare from the details you give us, for people living in England or Wales. You must sign it in front of a witness for it to be valid. We don't enrol deed polls with the courts. You can make your own deed poll for free.

Our promise

On Guided, Concierge and Couple plans, if a form we prepared is rejected because of a mistake we made, we'll fix it free. If we can't, we'll refund what you paid us.

Your responsibilities

Please check everything we prepare before you sign or send it, and tell us straight away if anything is wrong.
