What do I need to change my name?

Six quick questions, about two minutes. It's free, you don't need to sign up, and nothing you enter is sent to us unless you join the waitlist at the end.

1. What will your name be?
2. Where do you live?
3. Are you married yet?
4. Are you travelling abroad within 3 months of the wedding, or soon?
5. Do you own a car registered in your name?
6. Did you marry outside the UK?

Please answer every question.
